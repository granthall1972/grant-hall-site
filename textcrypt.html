Text Encrypt

Password-encrypt a message into a paste-safe block with AES-256-GCM. Runs entirely in your browser.

Message
Password
Confirm password
Encrypted block

AES-256-GCM, key from PBKDF2-SHA-256 (600,000 iterations), random salt and IV per message. A forgotten password cannot be recovered.
