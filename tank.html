Fish Tank

A chunky pixel reef rendered at 260×156 and blown up with scanlines for that cabinet-glow, 1983-in-an-arcade look. Add fish, sprinkle food, tap the glass to spook them.

Runs entirely in your browser. The whole scene is drawn on a tiny 260×156 buffer — every fish is a handful of hard pixels — then scaled up with a CRT scanline and vignette pass. Each species schools with its own kind, keeps its distance and drifts on a lazy wander. Tap or click the tank to drop food where you point; fish nearby chase it and any right on your finger bolt.
