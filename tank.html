Fish Tank

A little reef that runs on a canvas. Add fish, sprinkle food, tap the glass to spook them. They school, wander, dart and drift on their own.

Runs entirely in your browser. Each species schools with its own kind, keeps its distance, and drifts on a lazy wander. Tap or click the tank to drop a pinch of food where you point — fish nearby will chase it, and any right on top of your finger will bolt. Bigger, closer fish are drawn in front of smaller, further ones for a bit of depth.
