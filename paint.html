Paint

Pencil, shapes, text, and an image to draw on top of. Runs in the browser.

Size (px)
Border
Fill

Everything happens on this canvas in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Opening an image loads it onto the canvas as a flat background; there are no layers. The slashed swatch next to Border or Fill is transparent, so that part isn't painted and whatever is already on the canvas shows through. Text tool: pick it, click where you want the text, and type. Click away to place it, Escape to cancel. Zoom: scroll wheel over the canvas, or the magnifier (click to zoom in, Alt+click or right-click to zoom out). Middle-drag to pan. Ctrl+0, or click the magnifier again, to fit.
