Paint

Pencil, shapes, text, and an image to draw on top of. Runs in the browser.

Everything happens on this canvas in your browser. Nothing is uploaded. Opening an image loads it onto the canvas as a flat background; there are no layers. Text tool: click on the canvas, type, then click away or press Escape to cancel.
